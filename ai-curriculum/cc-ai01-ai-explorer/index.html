Understand AI and use generative AI responsibly and effectively.

Purpose

Build AI literacy, responsible-use habits and confidence using modern generative AI tools.

Core modules

  • What AI is and is not; everyday AI examples
  • Responsible AI, privacy, bias and verification
  • Prompt structure: role, context, task, constraints and output
  • Text, image and research-oriented generative AI workflows
  • Introduction to computational thinking and basic Python concepts

Learner outcomes

By the end of CC-AI01 you will be able to:

  • Explain core AI concepts in simple language
  • Create and refine useful prompts
  • Evaluate AI output instead of accepting it blindly
  • Complete a simple AI-powered mini project

Capstone project

AI-powered learning assistant, idea generator or simple interactive mini project.

Assessment & certification

  • Practical exercises and lab completion.
  • A graded capstone or applied project at every level.
  • Attendance and participation requirements defined for each cohort.

On completion you receive the CodenCode Certified AI Explorer certification.
