AI Curriculum Blueprint · 2027 pathway

The codencode.sg 6-level AI curriculum.

A structured, project-based pathway from AI literacy and coding foundations to production-style AI engineering, industry capstones and business transformation.

The codencode.sg AI pathway is a progressive six-level framework. Learners move from understanding and using AI (CC-AI01 AI Explorer) to building, deploying and leading AI projects (CC-AI06 AI Leader). Every level combines guided instruction, practical labs, assessment and a portfolio-ready capstone, and awards a CodenCode certification.

Assessment model

  • Practical exercises and lab completion.
  • A graded capstone or applied project at every level.
  • Portfolio evidence from Level 2 onward.
  • Code review, demonstration or presentation at advanced levels.
  • Attendance and participation requirements defined for each cohort.

How classes run

Classes are live and instructor-led, run online so you can join from anywhere in Singapore, and taught in English, Mandarin (中文) or Bahasa Melayu with Tamil-speaking support. Your first class is free. Every session is recorded and uploaded within 24 hours.
