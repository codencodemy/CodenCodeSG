CC-AI06 — AI Leader

Lead a real industry AI project from problem definition to presentation.

CC-AI06 AI Leader is Level 6 of the codencode.sg 6-level AI pathway in Singapore (Industry Capstone stage). Lead an AI project around a real organisational problem, balancing technology, users, governance and business value. It ends with a graded capstone — real industry capstone: problem → proposal → data → prototype → testing → presentation → deployment roadmap. — and the CodenCode Certified AI Leader certification.

Purpose

Lead an AI project around a real organisational problem, balancing technology, users, governance and business value.

Core modules

  • Problem discovery and requirements definition
  • AI opportunity assessment and solution selection
  • Project planning and team roles
  • AI governance, privacy, cybersecurity and responsible use
  • Prototype-to-pilot planning
  • Business case, value and adoption considerations
  • Stakeholder communication and client presentation
  • Entrepreneurship and product thinking

Learner outcomes

By the end of CC-AI06 you will be able to:

  • Translate a business problem into an AI project brief
  • Lead solution design and delivery
  • Defend technical and business decisions
  • Present a working capstone to an assessment panel
  • Produce technical documentation and an implementation roadmap

Capstone project

Real industry capstone: problem → proposal → data → prototype → testing → presentation → deployment roadmap.

Assessment & certification

  • Practical exercises and lab completion.
  • A graded capstone or applied project at every level.
  • Portfolio evidence from Level 2 onward.
  • Code review, demonstration or presentation at advanced levels.
  • Attendance and participation requirements defined for each cohort.

On completion you receive the CodenCode Certified AI Leader certification.
