CC-AI03 — AI Developer

Build AI applications using APIs, databases and introductory RAG.

CC-AI03 AI Developer is Level 3 of the codencode.sg 6-level AI pathway in Singapore (Application Development stage). Develop complete AI-enabled applications that use external models, databases and business data. It ends with a graded capstone — company knowledge assistant: upload documents, retrieve relevant information and answer grounded questions. — and the CodenCode Certified AI Developer certification.

Purpose

Develop complete AI-enabled applications that use external models, databases and business data.

Core modules

  • REST APIs and JSON
  • Application architecture and environment configuration
  • Databases and PostgreSQL fundamentals
  • Prompt engineering and structured outputs
  • Embeddings and semantic search concepts
  • Introduction to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
  • Basic authentication, logging and deployment concepts

Learner outcomes

By the end of CC-AI03 you will be able to:

  • Build an AI application with a backend and data layer
  • Store and retrieve application data
  • Ground model responses using supplied documents/data
  • Deploy and demonstrate a usable prototype

Capstone project

Company knowledge assistant: upload documents, retrieve relevant information and answer grounded questions.

Assessment & certification

  • Practical exercises and lab completion.
  • A graded capstone or applied project at every level.
  • Portfolio evidence from Level 2 onward.
  • Attendance and participation requirements defined for each cohort.

On completion you receive the CodenCode Certified AI Developer certification.
