How every session is structured

01

Discover

Identify participant roles, pain points and recurring tasks.

02

Understand

Teach the minimum AI concepts required for safe and effective use.

03

Demonstrate

Show workflows that resemble the client's actual work.

04

Practice

Participants complete guided exercises using realistic scenarios.

05

Build

Create reusable prompts, workflow templates or a small prototype.

06

Adopt

Define next steps, governance considerations and opportunities for implementation.

Assessment & evidence

  • Pre-training needs assessment where appropriate.
  • Hands-on exercises or scenario-based tasks.
  • End-of-session practical output or prototype.
  • Participant feedback and trainer evaluation.
  • Certificate of participation/completion where applicable.
  • For advanced programmes: team presentation or automation prototype review.
