What you'll learn

  • Phishing — how to spot scam emails, messages and fake login pages.
  • Password hygiene — strong passwords, password managers and multi-factor authentication.
  • Network basics — how home and office networks work and where they are weak.
  • Intro pentesting — a safe, guided first look at how security testers find vulnerabilities.

Why it matters in Singapore

Scams, many of them starting with a phishing message, make up a large share of reported crime in Singapore according to the Singapore Police Force's annual crime statistics. Basic security habits protect you, your family and your employer — and they are a sensible first step if you are curious about a cybersecurity career.

Who this cybersecurity course is for

  • Office workers and managers who handle company data or devices.
  • Parents and adults who want to protect themselves from online scams.
  • Beginners considering a career in cybersecurity.
